# Restricted area access policy

January 2026 revision. Questions: IT and Facilities.

The IT storeroom (reader ALMOX_TI) is a restricted area. Badge access is granted to the IT team, to Finance's Asset Management unit for the monthly check, to cleaning and to security. Every entry and exit is recorded. {#p-almox}

The storeroom is cleaned on business days, from 19:00, always by the same team. {#p-limpeza}

Security makes a night round of every restricted area at around 03:00, every day, weekends included. {#p-ronda}

Equipment only leaves the storeroom with a record in the asset system. Removal without a record is treated as loss. {#p-retirada}
