# Document system access policy

March 2025 version. Owners: managing partner and IT.

The archive is open to every active lawyer at the firm, including those on other matters, for looking up precedents and templates. Confidential documents follow the same rule: confidentiality applies outside the firm. {#p-acervo}

When anyone leaves the firm, HR notifies IT the same day, and IT disables the account and VPN access immediately. {#p-desligamento}

Remote access goes through the VPN, with the system username and password. {#p-vpn}

Links shared with clients expire after 48 hours and may only contain the version approved by the partner in charge. {#p-links}
