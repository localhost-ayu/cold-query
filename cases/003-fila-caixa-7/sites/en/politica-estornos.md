# Refund policy

October 2025 revision. Questions: regional management.

Refunds over R$ 50.00 need a store supervisor's password. Up to that amount, the cashier refunds alone, so the line keeps moving. {#p-limite}

A refund on a cash sale is paid back in cash, taken from that register's own drawer. {#p-dinheiro}

Every refund needs a reason and the original receipt. The cashier who makes the refund is recorded in the system, even if someone else made the sale. {#p-registro}

During promotion weeks, exchanges and cancellations follow the same rules, with a longer return window. {#p-promocao}
