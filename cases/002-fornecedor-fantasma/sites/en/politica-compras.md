# Purchasing and payments policy

August 2024 version. Owner: Controller's office.

Every new vendor is registered by Purchasing and needs the Controller's office approval before the first payment. Whoever registers a vendor cannot be its beneficiary. {#p-cadastro}

Every invoice needs a purchase order (PO) opened before it is issued. The only exception is reimbursement of small expenses, up to R$ 500.00. {#p-po}

The standard payment term is 28 days from the invoice date. Early payments require a written justification. {#p-prazo}

Payments up to R$ 30,000.00 are approved by the accounts payable team. Above that, by finance management. {#p-alcada}
